# Reload roadmap

Updated 2026-10-13. WhatsApp, Arabic/RTL, email OTP, knowledge, inbox, multiple-store routing and staff tools are implemented; they are not future-only prototype work.

## Current priorities

1. Salla public-app configuration/review, real merchant authorization, token-refresh/webhook lifecycle and verified product/order acceptance.
2. Stream Pay live acceptance and rollout: sandbox checkout, upgrades, payment records, signed callbacks and access checks are implemented. Monthly Standard SAR149 and Premium SAR299, annual SAR1,430.40 and SAR2,870.40 are approved; annual allowances refresh monthly; private first-month SAR99/199 applies selectively. Confirm bundle price, scheduled renewals/cancellation and live fees before enabling charging/enforcement.
3. Measure real per-store costs and usage, then set plan allowances, overages and trial limits; validate first paid journey before outreach to 100 merchants.
4. Bulk marketing: permitted contact list, approved templates, billing/limits check, campaign queue, delivery reporting, opt-out suppression and safe retry behavior.

## Acceptance and reliability

- Real matched-phone and mismatched-phone/email-OTP/manual-review customer journeys, merchant dashboard outcomes and optional photo evidence.
- Zid live variants, larger catalogues, performance/limits and written commercial-use permission.
- Confirm Meta template approvals and business/account limits; never infer from code or stale dashboard notes.
- Expand targeted diagnostic coverage, provider delivery/bounce handling, cost metering, capacity testing and backup/restore verification.
- Guided knowledge adoption and merchant-approved import accuracy; never auto-publish or self-train on unreviewed content.

## Later expansion

Merchant-owned WhatsApp numbers/Embedded Signup, Shopify, regulated financing partnerships, more operational analytics, and approved integrations for return logistics/refund payments. These are separate projects, not promised current features.
